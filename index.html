TikTok Content Agent

A private tool for one creator's own TikTok channel about childhood and family memories.

TikTok Content Agent helps the channel owner prepare short videos (script, AI voice-over, stock or AI-generated footage, subtitles). Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is uploaded. After approval, the tool uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to upload the video to the owner's own TikTok account, either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a post.

The tool is used only by its owner on their own account. It is not offered to the public and does not access other users' accounts.
