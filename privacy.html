Privacy Policy

TikTok Content Agent · Effective date: 7 November 2026

1. Summary

TikTok Content Agent is a private tool used only by its owner to upload approved videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from other TikTok users and does not sell or share data.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic account info (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, to identify the connected account.
  • Access and refresh tokens, needed to upload videos you approve (scopes video.upload, video.publish).
  • Upload status returned by TikTok for each video.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to upload videos that the account owner has approved and to check whether an upload succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and upload records are stored in a private database on the owner's own server and are never published or sent to third parties other than TikTok. API keys are kept in private environment files that are excluded from source control.

5. Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared. Data is sent only to TikTok, to perform the uploads you approve.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the TikTok connection is active. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions); stored tokens then stop working and are deleted on request. To request deletion, email the contact below.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
